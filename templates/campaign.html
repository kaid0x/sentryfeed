How to map responsibly

Map what the report describes, rather than what a group usually does. An explicit technique ID is a lead, not a reviewed finding. Choose a sub-technique only when the evidence supports that detail.

Tactics describe objectives. Their display order does not prove the order of an attack. Technique overlap does not establish attribution. Reviewed means you checked a mapping; it is not independent verification.

This first version covers Enterprise ATT&CK. It does not infer actors, generate detection rules, or claim defensive coverage.
