How connections get here

Stories mentioning the same organisations can become private review leads. A co-mention alone never creates a public branch. Before a connection is added, its direction, mechanism, scope and sources are reviewed. New records are approved locally and published with the next site update.

Incident months describe when activity occurred or was reported in the cited account, not the date we added it. A missing branch means we have not published that connection, not that no other organisations were affected.

Use this to understand supplier dependencies and incident response. The campaign workbench can help map a report's observed behaviour to ATT&CK.
