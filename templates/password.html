How your password stays private

  1. Your browser turns the password into a SHA-1 hash, a 40-character fingerprint, for example 5BAA61E4C9…
  2. Only the first 5 characters of that hash are sent to SentryFeed, which asks the breach database for every leaked hash starting with them, usually several hundred.
  3. Your browser compares that list with the rest of your hash itself. Neither SentryFeed nor the breach database ever sees your password or its full hash.
