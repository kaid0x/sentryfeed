What leaves this device

  1. In your browserThe password becomes a SHA-1 hash, 40 characters like 5BAA61E4C9…
  2. To SentryFeedThe first 5 characters only. SentryFeed asks the breach database for every leaked hash starting with them.
  3. Back in your browserThe returned list, usually several hundred hashes, is compared here. Nobody else sees the full hash.
