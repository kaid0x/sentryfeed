Redesign prototypes

Three directions, built on the same real data from this server's database. Each has a home page and one tool page (the password check), and each works on a phone. Links inside them lead to the current site's pages.

  1. A. Watch floor

    An operations dashboard built around one question: what needs attention now. A ranked queue with the reason for each item, the last 24 hours hour by hour, then the supporting panels.

    Cool greys so severity is the only warm colour. ENERGY 2, RHYTHM 2, MOTION 1.

  2. B. The Briefing

    A front page for cyber: the day in one sentence, one lead story, then sections for exploited flaws, the Gulf, markets and the week.

  3. C. Situation room

    The map is the page: pick a country to see its stories, switch between the world and the Gulf, and see plainly how many stories have no country.
