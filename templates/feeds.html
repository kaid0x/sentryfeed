Patch deadlines in your calendar

When a flaw that attackers are already using joins {{ ui.term("kev", "CISA's exploited list") }}, CISA gives US federal agencies a date to fix it by. Those deadlines aren't binding on anyone else, but they're a widely used yardstick for how urgent a fix is.

Subscribe, and they appear in Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar as all-day entries: one per day with a deadline, listing each flaw with a link to its page here, from the last {{ cal_past_days }} days and everything ahead. The entries never mark you as busy, and your calendar picks up new ones by itself.

How to subscribe

  • Google Calendar (on a computer): next to Other calendars, choose + and then From URL, and paste the link. Google checks subscribed calendars on its own schedule, so a new deadline can take up to a day to appear.
  • Outlook: Add calendar, then Subscribe from web, and paste the link.
  • Apple Calendar: File, then New Calendar Subscription, and paste the link.

How to follow a feed

  • A feed reader (Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire): add a new feed and paste the link.
  • Outlook: right-click RSS Subscriptions (or RSS Feeds), choose to add a new feed, and paste the link.
  • Slack: add Slack's RSS app to your workspace, then type /feed subscribe and the link in the channel that should get the stories.
