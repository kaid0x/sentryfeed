Your stack

Only the stories about the products you picked on My stack.

Unlike My stack, this list leaves your browser. It's part of the feed's link, so SentryFeed's server sees it each time your reader checks for new stories, and so does anyone you share the link with. SentryFeed doesn't log or store it.

Your products are kept in your browser, so building this link needs JavaScript. Without it, a link like {{ stack_base }}?stack=fortinet,exchange works too.

How to follow a feed

  • A feed reader (Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire): add a new feed and paste the link.
  • Outlook: right-click RSS Subscriptions (or RSS Feeds), choose to add a new feed, and paste the link.
  • Slack: add Slack's RSS app to your workspace, then type /feed subscribe and the link in the channel that should get the stories.
